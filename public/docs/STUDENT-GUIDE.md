# Student guide

## Get started

Open the campus link and sign in with the email your administrator enrolled. Complete your profile. If you do not see a course, ask the administrator to check both your enrollment and the Site access list. No payment is required by the app to create your profile.

Choose your batch using the selector near the top. The dashboard, progress and fees follow that selection.

## Your learning tools

- **Overview:** your course progress, classroom topics, next session and resources.
- **Live sessions:** class dates and meeting links; download an event to your calendar.
- **Recordings:** open class replays shared by the instructor.
- **Learning library:** notes, presentations and practical guides; filter or search them.
- **Practice labs:** read the task, write your solution and attach a file. Check instructor feedback and resubmit if asked.
- **Assessments:** daily, weekly and monthly tests. Locked tests become available after your instructor covers the topic.
- **My progress:** roadmap, completed resources, saved test results and a downloadable completion report.
- **Course & fees:** batch details, paid amount, balance and payment history.
- **Queries & concerns:** ask the team for help and read their reply.
- **Bootcamp guide:** course scope, learning process and expectations.

Mark a note, recording, slide deck or guide complete after studying it. This is self-reported completion. Labs are complete after instructor acceptance. Test scores are reported separately.

## Taking a test

Select Start test. Choose an answer for each question and move between questions. Submit before the timer ends. You have one attempt for each test in each batch. Closing the dialog does not pause the timer; reopening resumes the active attempt. Draft selections persist in the same browser tab’s session storage; server autosave supports resuming on another device; unsynced device drafts need the original tab.

Results and explanations are stored on the server. An expired attempt scores zero. For connection issues, contact the administrator; this release has no automatic attempt reset workflow.

## Files and privacy

Attach only the relevant solution or document. Never include passwords, cloud tokens, private keys or unrelated personal information. Files are limited to 20 MB. Other students cannot read your submissions or fees. Administrators can review submitted work.

## Android installation

1. Open the deployed campus link in Chrome on your Android phone.
2. Sign in and allow the page to finish loading.
3. Open Chrome’s menu (⋮) and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Launch **Arks Campus** from your home screen.

The campus adapts to phone screens; open the menu at the top left for navigation. Installation availability depends on browser and hosting support. This is a PWA, not a Play Store app. Learning data requires an internet connection. If offline, reconnect and reopen the campus.
